About关于我

An interdisciplinary perspective on sustainable development
以跨学科视角理解可持续发展

My academic interests lie at the intersection of environmental and resource economics, sustainability, and public policy. I am interested in how societies make decisions when economic development, environmental protection, and social well-being must be considered together.

Through postgraduate coursework at the University of Sydney, I have explored issues involving food and water security, public administration, global health policy, and community environmental change.

This website brings together my academic interests, selected coursework topics, professional memberships and citizen science contributions.

我的学术兴趣位于环境与资源经济学、可持续发展和公共政策的交叉领域。我关注的是，当经济发展、环境保护和社会福祉必须同时考虑时，社会应当如何作出决策。

在悉尼大学的研究生课程中，我接触了粮食与水资源安全、公共行政、全球卫生政策，以及社区环境变化等议题。

这个网站汇集了我的学术兴趣、部分课程项目、专业协会会员身份及公民科学贡献记录。
